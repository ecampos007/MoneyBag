Financial confidence for every student.

MoneyBag Finances is a prototype AI-powered coach that turns your income, life stage and goals into clear next steps, especially for HBCU students building wealth for the first time.

Financial literacy hasn't moved

It has stayed around 50% for the past 8 years. Personalized, interactive education can change that.

Who we serve

HBCU students are ambitious and often first-generation, yet many report high financial stress. We help them grow wealth and worry less about money.

What we offer

Tailored strategies, credit-building insights, saving options, and "what-if" scenarios to learn from before making real decisions.

Why MoneyBag

Existing tools each solve one piece. We combine them into education with deep personalization.

Intuit Assist

Built for taxes and accounting, and for people who already have a financial background.

Cleo

Great for budgeting and spending, but based on insights rather than future outlook.

Khan Academy

Free finance lessons, but static and not tailored to your income or lifestyle.

MoneyBag: personalized lessons, coaching, structured advice, and a long-term roadmap.

About us

Our mission: give every person guidance that fits their real situation, not generic financial advice.

What we're building

Money Bag Finances is developing an AI-powered prototype that adapts to each user. It looks at key personal factors, such as demographics, income level and life stage, to deliver guidance that fits their reality.

Why it matters

A college student just starting to build credit needs completely different recommendations than a young professional saving for a home or long-term investments. We turn complex concepts into clear steps users can take right away.
